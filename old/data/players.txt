# Player configuration.
# pci_rx / pci_rz : PCI contact-ellipse radii (feet). Bigger = easier to make contact.
# power_ev        : Maximum exit velocity (mph) on a perfect squared-up ball.
# side            : Batter handedness. R or L.
#
# trait_vert  : up | down | none  - rewards high or low contact (launch angle)
# trait_horiz : pull | push | none - rewards early (pull) or late (push/oppo) contact
# trait_bonus : how strong the trait's power swing is (0.0-0.3 is a sane range).
#   An "up + pull" hitter wants to swing slightly EARLY and UNDER the ball
#   (not late and on top of it) to get his bonus - matching a real pull-power hitter.
